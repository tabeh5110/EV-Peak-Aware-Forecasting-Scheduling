Using the Step 11D scenario file, we evaluated constrained EV charging scheduling across six forecast sites with 9079 calendar-aligned real empirical sessions and 12256 synthetic empirical sessions. The synthetic sessions were used only for robustness and scalability analysis and are not real six-site session logs. Under the main capacity_p95_plus_10percent capacity setting, uncertainty-aware scheduling reduced aggregate peak load from 558.36 kW to 367.45 kW (34.19%) while delivering 172926.06 kWh of 172927.12 kWh requested energy, leaving 0.001% unserved. Capacity violation hours changed from 484 under uncontrolled charging to 8 under uncertainty-aware scheduling. The final scheduling claim is strong scenario evidence: uncertainty-aware scheduling works across most sites and capacity scenarios with low unserved energy. These results should be interpreted as synthetic multi-site scenario evidence for scheduling robustness and scalability, not as real-world six-site session validation.
